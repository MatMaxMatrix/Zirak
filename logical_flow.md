### Logical Flow for Step-by-Step Breakdown:

1. **Initial Understanding:**
   - **Objective:** Understand the user's request and gather context.
   - **Actions:**
     - Analyze the user's query to determine the main objective.
     - Identify key action terms like 'analyze,' 'break down,' and 'steps.'

2. **Task Analysis:**
   - **Objective:** Break down the task into individual steps.
   - **Actions:**
     - Separate tasks into structured steps for a detailed breakdown.
     - Identify and extract key requirements from the user's request.

3. **Action Term Extraction:**
   - **Objective:** Extract key action terms for further analysis.
   - **Actions:**
     - Identify and extract key action terms like 'analyze,' 'break down,' and 'steps.'
     - Analyze the extracted text to determine the main focus.

4. **Detailed Breakdown:**
   - **Objective:** Provide a detailed breakdown of the task.
   - **Actions:**
     - Analyze the extracted steps to ensure precision and clarity.
     - Structure the steps in a logical flow for easy implementation.

5. **Review and Refinement:**
   - **Objective:** Review the logical flow for clarity and coherence.
   - **Actions:**
     - Ensure each step is clear, concise, and aligned with the user's request.
     - Make any necessary refinements to improve the overall flow and understanding.
